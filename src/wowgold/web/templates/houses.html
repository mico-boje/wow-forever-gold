Auction houses

Every ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplay, Roleplay PvP) has its own Alliance, Horde and Blackwater auction house, and they don't share listings or gold. The API lists all of them without saying which ruleset is which, so tell wowgold which one is yours.

Find your house

  1. In game, list any cheap item at an unusual price, for example 1 Linen Cloth for 12g 34s 56c (you can cancel it later; the deposit is tiny).
  2. Wait for the next snapshot (hourly; or run wowgold collect --once).
  3. Search for that exact listing: only your house has it.
